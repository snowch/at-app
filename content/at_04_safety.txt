Safety.

<break time="2.0s" />

Autogenic Training is gentle, and safe for most people. But it is a genuine tool, that reaches deep, and there are some things you must know before you begin.

<break time="1.5s" />

First, some conditions call for a teacher, or a therapist, rather than an app. If you live with psychosis, or schizophrenia, with severe depression, with a dissociative condition, with bipolar disorder in an active phase, or with active post-traumatic stress, please seek personal, professional guidance before practising on your own. Deep relaxation can bring up material that needs supported handling.

<break time="1.5s" />

Second, three of the exercises carry their own cautions, and the programme will remind you of these when you reach them. The heartbeat exercise, if you have a heart condition. The warm centre exercise, if you have diabetes, an abdominal condition, or are pregnant. And the cool forehead, if you are prone to migraines. When you reach each of these, take the caution seriously.

<break time="1.5s" />

Third, as your body settles, you may feel twitches, tingling, small jerks, waves of feeling, or a sense of floating, or sinking. These are normal. They are a sign the method is working, not a fault. Simply notice them, and return to the words. But if a wave of emotion is strong, or keeps returning, stop practising on your own, and seek support.

<break time="1.5s" />

And finally. This is not a substitute for medical, or psychological, treatment, and never a reason to change your medication. Only practise where it is safe to relax completely, and even to fall asleep. Never while driving, or operating machinery. If in doubt, ask your doctor before you begin.
