Welcome to the programme.

<break time="2.0s" />

This is a course in Autogenic Training. A method, developed a century ago, for teaching the body to relax itself, deeply, and on command.

<break time="1.5s" />

The aim is unusual. Not to give you a recording to lean on each day, but to teach you a skill you carry in yourself. By the end, you will be able to reach a state of deep calm, anywhere, in a matter of seconds, with no recording at all.

<break time="1.5s" />

You will learn six standard exercises, one at a time, over about ten weeks. Each one turns your quiet attention to a different part of the body. Heaviness in the limbs. Warmth. The calm of the heartbeat. The breath. A warm centre. And a cool, clear forehead.

<break time="1.5s" />

As you go, you will hear two voices. This one guides the teachings, the short talks, like this one, that introduce each exercise. A second voice leads the guided practices themselves.

<break time="1.5s" />

The pace is deliberate, and it is slow. You do not rush ahead. You stay with each exercise until it comes on its own, easily, before moving to the next.

<break time="1.5s" />

Three things make this work. Practise three times a day, every day, even if only for two minutes. Keep a short log, so you can see your progress. And, above all, learn the one attitude that the whole method rests upon. We turn to that, next. It is called passive concentration.

<break time="1.5s" />

But first, one matter of safety. Please listen to the safety teaching before you begin, and only practise where it is safe to relax completely, and even to fall asleep. Never while driving, or operating machinery.
