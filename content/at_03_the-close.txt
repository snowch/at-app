The close.

<break time="2.0s" />

This teaching is short, and it is the most important practical thing in the whole course. Please do not skip it.

<break time="1.5s" />

Autogenic Training produces a real drop in your arousal, and in the tone of your muscles. Your body genuinely slows down. If you stand up out of that state, without reversing it, you can feel groggy, sometimes lightheaded, occasionally shaky.

<break time="1.5s" />

So every session ends with a close. Also called a cancellation. It is brisk, deliberate, and always the same.

<break time="1.5s" />

First, say the ending phrase, the same one every time, so that it becomes a switch. Arms firm. Breathe deeply. Eyes open.

<break time="1.5s" />

Then, make fists, and release them, firmly, three or four times.

<break time="1.5s" />

Bend and straighten the arms, vigorously, two or three times.

<break time="1.5s" />

Take one deep, deliberate breath.

<break time="1.5s" />

And open the eyes.

<break time="1.5s" />

That is the close. There is one, single exception. If you are practising in bed, to fall asleep, you leave the close out, and let yourself drift. In every other case, without exception, you close the session. Even if you were only lying down, and only for a minute.
