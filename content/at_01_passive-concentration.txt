Passive concentration.

<break time="2.0s" />

This is the heart of the whole method. If you understand only one thing, understand this.

<break time="1.5s" />

In Autogenic Training, you are not commanding your body. You are not forcing a feeling into being. And you are not checking, anxiously, to see whether it has worked.

<break time="1.5s" />

Instead, you hold a short phrase, lightly, in your attention. And you let whatever happens, happen. Including nothing.

<break time="1.5s" />

Take the first phrase you will use. My right arm is heavy. This is not a command, to make the arm heavy. It is closer to simply noticing, in the direction of heaviness. You turn your attention towards the arm, you offer the words, and then you wait, without demanding anything.

<break time="1.5s" />

Here is the strange truth at the centre of it all. The sensations arrive only when you stop requiring them. The moment you try, and strain, and check, you push them away. The moment you let go of the outcome, they begin to come.

<break time="1.5s" />

So when you catch yourself trying, and you will, again and again, for the first two weeks, the correction is not to try harder, in a more relaxed way. It is to let go of the result, completely, and return, gently, to the words.

<break time="1.5s" />

An indifferent attitude, towards whether it works, is not a nice extra. It is the very thing that makes it work. You are not learning six phrases. You are learning this one, quiet, uncaring stance. The phrases are only the vehicle.
