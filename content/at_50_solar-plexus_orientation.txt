Exercise five. The warm centre.

<break time="2.0s" />

The fifth exercise brings warmth to the soft centre of the body, below the breastbone, above the navel. The old name for this region is the solar plexus.

<break time="1.5s" />

The phrase is. My solar plexus is warm. If that is hard to make sense of, my abdomen is warm, or, warmth is flowing through my abdomen, work just as well.

<break time="1.5s" />

As with the warmth of the limbs, resting a hand on the area, for the first few sessions, helps you find it.

<break time="1.5s" />

Please take this caution seriously, before you begin. Speak to your doctor first if you have diabetes, as abdominal warmth can affect digestion, and alter your insulin needs. Also with active ulcers, any acute abdominal condition, recent abdominal surgery, or if you are pregnant. And know this. The warm centre can be left out of the sequence entirely, without breaking it. If in any doubt, skip it, and move on to the forehead.

<break time="1.5s" />

What it feels like. A spreading, internal warmth. Often with audible sounds from the gut. These are a good sign, not an embarrassment.
