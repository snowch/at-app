The shape of a session.

<break time="2.0s" />

Every session, from your very first to your last, has the same five parts. Only the middle one grows.

<break time="1.5s" />

First, position. You take one of three postures. Sitting on the edge of a chair, slumped but balanced, hands hanging between the knees. Or in an armchair, head resting back, arms along the arms of the chair. Or lying on your back, arms a little away from the body, feet falling open. In all three, nothing is crossed, nothing is clenched, and no muscle has to work to hold you there.

<break time="1.5s" />

Second, settle. The eyes close. Two or three slow breaths, simply noticed, not controlled. Then a brief, passive sweep through the body, from the face down to the feet, changing nothing. This takes only twenty or thirty seconds.

<break time="1.5s" />

Third, the formula work. This is the part that grows. Here you work through the exercise you are learning now, and all the ones you have learned before it. This is where the programme lives.

<break time="1.5s" />

Fourth, the close. Also called the cancellation. This reverses the deep relaxation before you stand up, and it is not optional. We give it a teaching of its own, next.

<break time="1.5s" />

And fifth, the log. Thirty seconds, to note what happened. This is the part learners skip, and it is the part that makes real progress possible.

<break time="1.5s" />

Position. Settle. Formula. Close. Log. The same shape, every time.
