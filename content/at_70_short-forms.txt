The short forms.

<break time="2.0s" />

The full sequence is not the goal. The goal is to reach the calm state quickly, anywhere. Once you have trained the full form, three shorter forms open up to you.

<break time="1.5s" />

The full form. Ten to fifteen minutes. All six exercises, in sequence. Once a day, ideally.

<break time="1.5s" />

The short form. Two or three minutes. Only the collapsed phrases, one after another. Arms and legs, heavy and warm. Heartbeat calm and regular. It breathes me. Solar plexus warm. Forehead pleasantly cool. Neck and shoulders heavy. I am at peace. One or two repetitions each.

<break time="1.5s" />

And the instant form. About thirty seconds. A single, whole phrase, and a couple of breaths, used in the moment. Before a difficult call. In a queue. Between meetings. This one only works once the full form is trained. It is a trigger, for a response you have already built. Not a shortcut past building it.

<break time="1.5s" />

One thing does not shorten. The close is still required, after the short form, and after the instant form. Only in bed, drifting to sleep, do you leave it out.
