Settle into your posture. Sitting, or lying. Nothing crossed. Nothing held.

<break time="3.0s" />

Let your eyes gently close.

<break time="3.0s" />

Two or three slow breaths. Not controlled. Simply noticed.

<break time="6.0s" />

And now a brief, passive sweep, through the body. The face. The jaw. The shoulders. The arms, and the hands. The chest, and the belly. The hips. The legs, and the feet. Changing nothing.

<break time="6.0s" />

We begin. Say to yourself, quietly. I am completely calm.

<break time="5.0s" />

Now turn your attention to your right arm. And offer the words, lightly, without forcing anything.

<break time="2.0s" />

My right arm is heavy.

<break time="5.0s" />

My right arm is heavy.

<break time="6.0s" />

My right arm is heavy.

<break time="7.0s" />

Let the words fade, and simply notice the arm. Let whatever is there, be there.

<break time="8.0s" />

Now, if you have reached this stage, turn to the left arm. My left arm is heavy.

<break time="6.0s" />

My left arm is heavy.

<break time="7.0s" />

And both arms, together. Both arms are heavy.

<break time="6.0s" />

Both arms are heavy.

<break time="8.0s" />

And when the legs are part of your practice, let the heaviness spread. Both legs are heavy.

<break time="6.0s" />

Arms and legs are heavy.

<break time="8.0s" />

Rest, now, in the weight of the body. Nothing to do.

<break time="8.0s" />

And gently, once more. I am completely calm.

<break time="6.0s" />

Now we close. Make fists, and release them. Firmly. Three or four times.

<break time="3.0s" />

Bend, and straighten the arms. Vigorously.

<break time="3.0s" />

Take one deep, deliberate breath.

<break time="2.0s" />

And open your eyes. The session is complete.
