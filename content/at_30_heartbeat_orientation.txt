Exercise three. Heartbeat.

<break time="2.0s" />

The third exercise turns your attention, quietly, to the heart. Not to change it. Simply to let it become calmly present.

<break time="1.5s" />

The phrase is. My heartbeat is calm and regular.

<break time="1.5s" />

Handle this one with care. Attending to the heart makes some people uneasy, or too aware of it. If it brings unease, back off. Spend another week on warmth, and return to it later. If it brings unease every time, this is a stage to work through with a therapist, rather than an app.

<break time="1.5s" />

To find the beat, rest your right hand lightly over the heart, or on the upper abdomen, for the first few sessions. Once the rhythm is easy to find, let the hand return to rest.

<break time="1.5s" />

Do not, ever, try to slow it. The phrase is calm and regular. Not slow. Any attempt to change the rate is the opposite of the exercise.

<break time="1.5s" />

What it feels like. The beat becoming quietly present, and unremarkable. Neither hidden, nor intrusive. Simply there.

<break time="1.5s" />

A caution. If you have any heart condition, an irregular rhythm, or a recent cardiac event, speak to your doctor before beginning this exercise.

<break time="1.5s" />

This week also introduces a second, gentler formula. The neck and shoulders. Give the heartbeat three or four days on its own, first. Then add it.
