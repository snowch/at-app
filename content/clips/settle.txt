First, settle into your position.

<break time="1.5s" />

Lie on your back, somewhere firm and comfortable, or sit well supported in a chair.

<break time="2.0s" />

Let your legs fall a little apart, and rest your arms a little away from your sides, whichever way is easy.

<break time="2.0s" />

Let yourself be symmetrical, and fully supported, so that no muscle needs to hold you.

<break time="2.0s" />

Take one slow breath, and let it go.

<break time="2.0s" />

And when you are ready, let your eyes gently close.
