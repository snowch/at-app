Exercise six. The cool forehead.

<break time="2.0s" />

The sixth, and last, exercise brings a light coolness to the forehead. It completes the picture. The body heavy, and warm. The head clear, and cool. This is the characteristic state of Autogenic Training, and it is why the method sharpens the mind, as well as calming the body.

<break time="1.5s" />

The phrase is. My forehead is pleasantly cool. The word pleasantly is doing real work. Do not drop it. Unqualified coolness can tip into something unpleasant, or, in some people, bring on a headache.

<break time="1.5s" />

Keep this one brief. Noticeably shorter than the others. Two or three repetitions, rather than five or six, especially at first. It is the exercise most linked to headache, when overdone.

<break time="1.5s" />

A caution. If you are prone to migraines, or frequent headaches, approach this one gently, keep it very brief, and stop at once if it brings any head pain.

<break time="1.5s" />

What it feels like. A faint coolness across the brow, as though air were moving. Often so subtle that learners assume they have failed. Here, subtle is normal. Do not reach for more.
