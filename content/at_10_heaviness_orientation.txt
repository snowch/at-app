Exercise one. Heaviness.

<break time="2.0s" />

The first exercise turns your quiet attention to the weight of the limbs. As the muscles let go, the arms and legs begin to feel heavy. This is real muscular relaxation, felt as weight, or sinking.

<break time="1.5s" />

The phrase you will use is simple. My right arm is heavy. If you are left-handed, begin with the left. You start with the dominant arm, because it responds first.

<break time="1.5s" />

You do not add everything at once. For the first few days, the dominant arm alone. Then the other arm, and both arms together. Then, over the following week, the dominant leg, the other leg, and both legs. And finally, the collapsed phrase. Arms and legs are heavy. The programme will guide this expansion.

<break time="1.5s" />

What does heaviness feel like? It varies, and all of it counts. Weight. Sinking. A thickening. The limb feeling larger than it is, or hard to locate, or merged with what it rests on. Some people feel warmth before heaviness. Accept it. Do not correct it.

<break time="1.5s" />

The one common error is tensing the arm, to try to feel it. If you can feel the muscle working, you have left passive concentration. Remember, you are not making the arm heavy. You are only noticing, in the direction of heaviness, and letting it come.
